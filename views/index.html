Welcome to the Archive

It started with whispers in the walls, a scratching that no one could trace. The building had been abandoned for decades, its halls filled with the stale air of secrets. Those who entered never returned the same. Some say the floors shift beneath your feet; others claim they saw faces forming in the plaster. The last journal recovered read:

"They are not shadows. They eat light. When night falls the portraits blink and the candles bleed. I write this with hands that tremble, hoping some future soul will burn these pages and close the door for good. If you have found this, flee. Do not stay. They know your name."

A thin chill runs through the spine of anyone who reads it, as if the house itself inhales.
